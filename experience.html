Data Science
03

Pacific Life Re

Data Science & Analytics · Reinsurance

My first proper experience working as a Data Scientist — and where I learnt what data science looks like outside the classroom.

Over the year, I worked across data engineering, machine learning, statistical analysis and AI, tackling problems that often required me to teach myself new approaches along the way.

A few lessons that stuck with me:

  • Understand the data before the model.Good EDA and careful feature preparation can matter more than using a sophisticated algorithm.
  • The model isn't the end of the job.The real challenge is interpreting results and deciding what they actually mean for the business.
  • Learn independently, but don't work in isolation.I became comfortable researching unfamiliar problems myself while knowing when to ask questions or challenge my assumptions.
  • Communication matters.A technically correct analysis isn't useful if the people around you can't understand or trust the conclusion.

Most importantly, I realised that I really enjoy the combination of logic, technical problem-solving, analysis and judgement that comes with being a Data Scientist.

An idea I’m excited about
What if insurance underwriting felt more like a conversation?

Traditional insurance applications rely heavily on long forms and predefined questions. But people's answers don't always fit neatly into those boxes — and the resulting data can be difficult to analyse.

I'm interested in whether an AI underwriting agent could create a more dynamic experience.

Instead of asking everyone the same questions, the agent could adapt its next question based on the answers it receives — much like a human underwriter would. Straightforward cases could move quickly, while unusual answers could trigger additional questions automatically.

That could potentially create a better experience for customers and produce more structured, useful data for insurers.

It's an idea that sits at the intersection of several things I've become interested in through my work: AI, data quality, product design and decision-making.
