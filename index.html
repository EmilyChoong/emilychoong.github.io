Info

Hi, I’m Emily! I study Data Science at the University of Bristol and have recently completed a year-long placement in the R&D department of a reinsurance company.

About

I’ve always enjoyed thinking logically and solving problems, which is a big part of why I enjoy Data Science. I’m particularly interested in how we can use AI and machine learning to uncover patterns, extract insights and make better-informed decisions from data. With technology developing so rapidly, the possibilities for what we can do with data are constantly expanding — and I’m excited to explore them.

Working it out

A big part of my experience so far has been learning to tackle problems I’ve never encountered before. From building an NLP system to standardise free-text insurance claims, to working with over a billion rows of UK healthcare data, I’ve learnt that the most important skill isn’t always knowing the answer — it’s knowing how to work towards one.

What I enjoy

I enjoy work that gives me a mix of analysis, problem-solving and building, and how AI and data can be used to create better products and experiences.

Outside of work

Outside of work, you’ll usually find me running to bakeries around London, doing hot yoga, or reading.
